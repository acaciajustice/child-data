# ORR Unaccompanied Children Historical Monthly Data

This resource provides an archive of monthly data on unaccompanied children (UC) passing through the custody of the Office of Refugee Resettlement (ORR), an office within the Administration for Children and Families (ACF), part of the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS). The dataset includes demographic information and metrics on facility capacity, referrals, and discharges from ORR custody, as originally published by ORR.  

ORR makes these data publicly available, but its online resources do not include data from prior fiscal years. The Acacia Center for Justice provides this historical data here, compiled and standardized in an accessible format. 

## Files Available for Download 
The archived dataset presents ORR's monthly data on unaccompanied children, from October 2017 through September 2026 (Fiscal Years 2018–2026).  As data from additional complete fiscal years become available, the dataset will be updated accordingly.  
For up-to-date monthly data for the current fiscal year, visit ORR's official [UACB Data page](https://acf.gov/orr/research-data/uac)  for data on unaccompanied children.

## Data Dictionary 
The dataset is structured as a month-level time series, with each reported statistic provided as a column. Each statistic is labeled with a **Field Name**, a short name generated by Acacia. **ORR Source Table** indicates the original ORR table from which a particular statistic was drawn. **Field Label** is a short description of what the statistic represents; where available, Acacia used labels as provided by ORR, but in some instances Acacia's Field Labels add additional information for clarity. **Field Definition** is an explanation of how the statistic is calculated, drawn from ORR's table sub headers, footnotes, and Acacia's working knowledge of ORR public data.

### Notes 
Notes beginning with **"ORR"** were provided alongside the official data published online. The listed fiscal years indicate the annual tables for which these notes appeared.  

[1]: **ORR** (FYs 18-25): Includes Influx shelters  

[2]: **ORR** (FYs 22-23): ORR estimates that 30% of the operational capacity is unavailable due to COVID-19 restrictions. The percentage varies from program to program and is generally affected by quarantine needs and staff availability  

[3]: **ORR** (FYs 18-25): Includes all discharge types and all program types. *For FY 2026 data, ORR's public reporting misaligns this footnote with the wrong data row; that is corrected here*  

[4]: **ORR** (FYs 18-25): Length of Care is calculated as of the last day of the corresponding month shown above. *For FY 2026 data, ORR's public reporting incorrectly applies this footnote to both Length of Care Statistics*    

[5]: ORR's documentation does not clarify which 30 days are used in calculations for months with 31 days, nor whether fewer than 30 days are used for February calculations.  

[6]: Discharge reasons include releases to sponsors, age-outs, deportations, voluntary departures, and other circumstances.

[7]: The title for this source table was *Gender* from FYs 18-22, *Sex* from FYs 23-24, and is now *UAC Sex Data*  

[8]: For detailed information about ORR facility types, see [Section 1.2](https://acf.gov/orr/policy-guidance/uac-bureau-policy-guide) of ORR's UC policy guide  

[9]: From FYs 18-20, ORR reported a single count of tender age children in Therapeutic facilities; this is archived in the "num_tender_age_therapeutic" field. Beginning in FY 21, ORR replaced that field with a separate counts of tender age children in Therapeutic Staff Secure and Therapeutic Group Home facilities  

[10]: Sponsor categories (i.e., Category 1,2,3) are defined in [Section 2.1](https://acf.gov/orr/policy-guidance/uac-program-policy-guide-section-2#2.2.1) of ORR's UC policy guide

## About the Data 
All data in this repository is derived from public data made available by ORR.  
### History of Data Availability 
- Previously, monthly UC data were published at HHS’s [Unaccompanied Children Information](https://www.hhs.gov/programs/social-services/unaccompanied-children/index.html) website, on separate pages for each fiscal year going back to Fiscal Year 2018. These pages were all named by the fiscal year reviewed, e.g., “Latest UC Data – FY 2018.” The final update to the HHS website’s monthly UC data resource added data for February 2025. 

- In April 2025, UC data were removed from the HHS website. During an update to ORR’s [Unaccompanied Alien Children](https://acf.gov/orr/programs/uac) website, monthly data from Fiscal Year 2025 were consolidated with other annual UC data on its Fact Sheets and Data page. That page was previously titled "Data".

- In November 2025, when October 2025 monthly data—the start of Fiscal Year 2026—were posted online, data from Fiscal Year 2025 were removed.

- In November 2026, when October 2026 monthly data—the start of Fiscal Year 2027—were posted online, data from Fiscal Year 2026 were removed.

- As of November of 2026, ORR's [UACB Data](https://acf.gov/orr/research-data/uac) page presents monthly data from Fiscal Year 2027 only. Monthly UC data from prior fiscal years is not available on government websites.

### Data Sources   

The following sources were used to compile the archived dataset: 
1. **Internet Archive (Wayback Machine)**: Archived snapshots of HHS’s “Latest UC Data” and ORR's “Data” pages.
2. **ORR's UACB Data page**: Monthly data for Fiscal Year 2026.
3. **Acacia Center for Justice Internal Archives**: Records of ORR's published data on unaccompanied children kept by Acacia staff. 

### Validation Methodology 
To ensure the accuracy of the archived dataset, Acacia employed a multi-step validation approach:   
1. Acacia's internal records of ORR's published historical data were used to establish a baseline dataset, spanning FY2018 through FY2025.
2. Two researchers independently reconstructed the historical dataset using copies of ORR's website archived on the Internet Archive. A complete catalog of specific archived URLs and collection dates is available upon request.
3. The two newly reconstructed datasets were compared to the original baseline dataset. Using systematic validation functions, each data point was evaluated across all three datasets. Values matching across all three datasets received a "match" flag, and any discrepancies were automatically flagged as "no match" for manual review.
4. Both researchers who performed the independent reconstructions collaboratively reviewed all flagged discrepancies. For each "no match case, the team identified the source of discrepancy, cross-referenced the original archived ORR source, determined the correct value, and documented the resolution.
5. After ORR completed its record of FY2026 data posted online, Acacia incorporated FY2026 data into this archived dataset.
